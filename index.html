Controls
Drag: move · Wheel: zoom (the view tilts as you get closer)
Right-drag or Shift+drag: rotate and tilt
WASD move · Q/E rotate · T/G tilt · Z/X zoom
Left-click: select (click again to cycle)
Right-click: move / attack
A city that grows asks where its new citizen goes: click a highlighted tile (Esc: the governor decides at end of turn)
B found city · F fortify · P pillage · Space skip · R rally · U deploy
Research, Politics and Identity: click their tracks in the top bar · Government, Religion, Diplomacy, Log: the buttons (Esc closes)
N/Tab next unit · C center · Enter end turn
H hex grid on/off · V walk on the surface (first person) · J debug views (height map, water)
